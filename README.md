🎤 Political Showdown: World at War

A rhythm game where world politics meets musical battles!

Political Showdown: World at War is a political-themed rhythm game inspired by classic music battle games. Choose your political character, face opponents from around the world, and prove your rhythm skills in intense musical showdowns!

🌎 About the Game

Enter an arena where political leaders face off in rhythm-based battles. From Brazilian politics to international rivalries, every battle brings a new challenge.

Choose your character, select your difficulty, configure your controls, and get ready to fight for victory!

🎮 Features
Playable Characters: Choose which political leader you want to control.
Multiple Opponents: Face different political figures from around the world.
Difficulty Levels: Choose between different difficulty settings.
Custom Controls: Play using WASD, arrow keys, or D/F/J/K.
Rhythm Gameplay: Hit the arrows at the right moment to score points.
Health System: Missing notes costs energy, with penalties depending on difficulty.
Scoring System: Build combos and achieve high scores.
Full-Screen Gameplay: Enjoy battles across your entire browser window.
🕹️ Controls
Action	Keys
Left	A / D / ←
Down	W / F / ↓
Up	S / J / ↑
Right	D / K / →

Choose your preferred control scheme before starting a battle.

⚙️ Difficulty Levels

The game features different difficulty settings that affect:

Note speed.
Number of arrows.
Frequency of notes.
Penalties for missed notes.

Higher difficulties demand faster reactions and greater precision!

🛠️ Technologies

The game is built using:

HTML5
CSS3
JavaScript
HTML Canvas
Web Audio API

No external game engine is required.

🚀 How to Play
Download or clone this repository.
Open the project folder.
Open index.html in your browser.
Choose your character.
Select your preferred controls.
Pick a difficulty level.
Start the battle!
🎯 Objective

Hit the incoming arrows in rhythm, maintain your combo, preserve your energy, and defeat your opponent!
